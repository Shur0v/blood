## Content and source review

The component/local-brief inventory includes the exact approved English copy, reviewed Urdu place labels, geographic sources, city references, health resources and stored recipes. City references were checked against GeoNames Pakistan data. Its coordinates and the 30-km disambiguation check are not administrative boundaries.

The AJK guide describes Pakistan-administered Azad Jammu and Kashmir, not all Kashmir. Broad Punjab or Kashmir records are not assigned to a city. Legacy region-labelled directories state that their own counts describe the recorded label rather than the curated regional aggregate. City search remains separate from regional figures.

Punjab/Sindh authority directories appear only in the relevant jurisdiction. Federal health and transplant-authority references do not imply affiliation, guaranteed availability or a new booking integration. External authority sites may be unavailable; no medical decision depends on their being a live BloodNet service.

No hospital rosters, shortages, response times, success stories, medical reviewers or partnerships were invented. Private-profile examples are labelled fictional. English remains the primary language; Urdu place recognition does not establish translated pages or guarantee Urdu-query rankings. Public content contains no private neighborhoods, precise donor coordinates or medical attachments.

## Owner deployment and publication

1. Run the existing `bash ./deploy-prod.sh` procedure from `/var/www/bloodnet`; verify the additive migration and application health checks.
2. Open Dashboard Overview → **Pakistan Local Pages** as an active administrator. No new environment setting or paid API is required.
3. Review private previews for Karachi, Lahore and Punjab. Check current local mappings, source figures, official destinations, privacy and the practical usefulness of the text.
4. Save drafts and explicitly publish reviewed pilots. New editorial guides intentionally return 404 and stay out of public sitemaps until published. Existing eligible directory routes receive their new presentation through deployment.
5. Verify the actual domain on real mobile/desktop devices: styles, canonical destinations, registration/login, filters, sixteen-card pagination, source labels, contact dialogs, requests where enabled, `/pk`, bottom menu and unchanged regional landing pages.
6. Review and publish remaining guides individually. Monitor crawl errors, impressions by page/query/language, registrations and contact initiation. Neither publication nor unique design guarantees ranking or AI recommendations.

Withdraw removes a curated guide from public eligibility; Restore also clears its override draft. Existing directories use inherited defaults and retain their original coverage gating. Keep the additive table and managed assets when rolling application code back. Do not delete donor data as a rollback step.
