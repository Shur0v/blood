# Regional profile height units

Profiles in Bangladesh and other Asian countries default to inches. The height summary also shows feet and inches, for example `66 in (5 ft 6 in)`. Europe, the USA and other countries default to centimetres. These are BloodNet display preferences, not claims about a country's official measurement system.

Administrators can change this under **Overview → Regional page content, design & images → choose country / optional city → Regional profile settings → Height unit**. Choose the regional default, inches or centimetres, enable publication and save regional content. A published city unit overrides its country; a city without an explicit unit inherits the published country preference. Unpublished entries have no effect. Existing account profiles refresh the preference when reopened or the browser regains focus.

The profile's saved primary country and city determine its unit, including when a staff member opens that profile. Travel or browser geolocation does not change the unit. Saved health-data heights continue to use centimetres, including existing records. Inch slider edits convert to centimetres before saving. Changing the preference alone never rewrites a height or changes its unknown/not-set state. No database migration or conversion of existing records is required.
