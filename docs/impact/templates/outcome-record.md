# Outcome evidence template — restricted when completed

Use for voluntary, minimal outcome follow-up. This is a collection guide, not an in-app feature or a clinical record. Keep completed forms, participant identities and evidence outside Git and public issues. Decide the restricted storage location, authorized reviewers and retention/review period before collecting responses.

## Invitation and consent

Suggested invitation: “We are documenting whether BloodNet helped people make donor connections. Participation is optional and will not affect access to BloodNet. You may skip questions or ask us to correct or withdraw your account of the event. Please do not send medical documents. We will use an approved aggregate summary; we will ask separately before publishing your name, quotation or story.”

- Consent to this follow-up and aggregate reporting: [yes/no; date/method; scope explained]
- Separate permission to contact a corroborating organisation/person: [yes/no; scope]
- Separate permission for any identifiable public story: [not requested / yes / no; exact approved content]
- Private contact/withdrawal route explained: [method; keep contact details in a separate restricted record]

If consent is declined, stop collection. Explain any limits on withdrawing information already published in non-identifying aggregate form; do not promise removal from historical copies you cannot control.

## Minimum event record

- Random internal event reference: [no patient ID, name, phone or email]
- Broad event date/period and type: [blood donation / other; avoid unnecessary identifying detail]
- Participant's description of BloodNet's role: [minimal non-clinical account]
- Stage: [reported donor contact / reported donation / independently corroborated donation]
- Source type and received date: [participant report / authorized independent confirmation]
- Duplicate check: [linked internal event references; same event counted once]
- Review status / reviewer / date: [unreviewed / reviewed / disputed / withdrawn]
- Restricted corroboration reference and permissions: [only if applicable]

Do not request diagnoses, blood test results, medical reports, prescription images, identity documents or unnecessary donor/recipient details as proof. A willingness pledge or request is not a completed donation. A reported organ-related event is not a verified transplant.

## Corroboration and publication

Independently corroborated status requires a reviewer to record who was authorized to confirm the event, what non-clinical fact was confirmed, when, and under which permission. A screenshot of traffic or a directory listing is insufficient. Leave unverified facts unverified.

- Approved aggregate inclusion: [yes/no; reviewer/date]
- Re-identification review: [remove small-location/date breakdowns or identifying details]
- Public story approval, if any: [exact version and consent review date]
- Evidence retention/deletion review date and owner: [values]
- Correction/withdrawal action: [date; private record updated; aggregate/story correction as applicable]

Stages can overlap for one event; do not add them into a success total. Publish no claim of lives saved or sole causal attribution based on this record. Use the [monthly report](monthly-report.md) for approved aggregate findings.
